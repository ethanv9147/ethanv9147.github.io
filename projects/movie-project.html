Movie Project

Context

With one other person, we were assigned with the task to make up a plot to a fictional movie as well as make it based off of a certain genre or genres. Various additions were required to be included such as backgrounds, fonts, links, divs,spans, classes, and many more. After carefully deciding on what to do, me and my partner came to the conclusion that we wanted to work on a scifi genre movie about a person who gets stuck in space and the movie would be called "A spec in stars".

Content

For the movie project that both me and my partner were making, we needed to make a plan so that the workload could be equally distributed and to also maintain proper organization. We had one person work on coming up with the story which was basically about an astronaut becoming stuck in space then found by an extraterrestrial army and then the astronaut eventually notices that they had eventually turned into a star. One person wrote it, both people styled it, one person added classes, and so on. The overall story was meant to make it unique when compared to the typical scifi film, the movie was meant to be unpredictable, fun, and leaving the reader yearning for more.

Reflection

During the process of making and structuring this project me and my partner ran into various errors. These mainly conssisted of forgetting where to correctly place divs and classes. Additionally there were some issues with sucessfully importing fonts because sometimes a font I imported would not apply to the text that I selected. I eventually saw that there was another link which I did not originally notice that was interfering with the link that was importing the font.
